# stillstamp Support

- Product and technical support: help@akra.kr
- Privacy questions: help@akra.kr
- Purchase and credit recovery: link the Google account used for payment, then
  choose **Restore purchases** in the shop. Do not repurchase while recovery is pending.
- Local postcard deletion: delete an item from **My postcards**, or clear app
  data/uninstall stillstamp to remove all local records.
- Advertising privacy choices: use the in-app entry when shown and the device's
  advertising controls where applicable.
- Cloud identity and wallet deletion: choose **Delete cloud account** on the
  home screen. It deletes the anonymous identity and AKRA-controlled wallet,
  ledger, rewarded-receipt, generation, and temporary-result records. Locally
  saved postcards remain. If interrupted, reopen the app and use the retry
  action; cloud features stay locked until deletion finishes.

When contacting support, include the app version, operating-system version, the
affected screen, and a short description. Do not send photos, passwords,
Firebase tokens, private keys, payment credentials, or complete advertising
receipts. No response-time commitment has been approved yet.

The in-app path is a release candidate until it passes signed-device testing and
the matching public HTTPS deletion instructions are published.

Account recovery covers server credits and ad-free time only. The postcard
library stays on the device, so save postcards to the photo library before
clearing app data or uninstalling.
